- You can now reorder the play queue: hold a track and drag it to where it should play. A small round button with a cross takes a track out of the queue (the track that is playing is replaced by the next one).
- The same in your own playlists: hold and drag to reorder, the cross removes a track from the playlist. Both are saved right away. (Albums, artists and the smart playlists are built from the library, so they stay as they are.)
- The ⋮ menu is gone from the queue and from your own playlists: its library actions (add to playlist, ringtone, share, delete file) did nothing in the queue. They are still in the library and in the player.
- Playlists, albums and artists no longer show a meaningless "0×" play counter on every track.

**APK notes:** signed with the same project key as before, so it installs over the previous version and keeps your data. arm64-v8a only, minimum Android 9 (API 28).
