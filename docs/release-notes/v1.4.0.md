- You can now reorder the play queue: the ⋮ menu of a queue row has Move up, Move down and Remove from queue. Removing the track that is playing continues with the next one.
- The same for your own playlists: Move up, Move down and Remove from playlist, saved right away. (Albums, artists and the smart playlists are built from the library, so they stay as they are.)
- The queue's menu no longer offers the library actions (add to playlist, ringtone, share, delete file) that did nothing there.
- Playlists, albums and artists no longer show a meaningless "0×" play counter on every track.
